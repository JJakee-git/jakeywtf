# Project content audit

Updated November 2, 2026. The site now contains 25 project pages: 22 with project descriptions and three artwork-only pages. Fourteen projects were added to the Work canvas, and five existing placeholders were developed into sourced case studies. The three original Figma case studies retain their imported artwork, copy, and interactions.

## Scope of the search

Searched design coursework within `D:/00 Chapman Work`, including the primary portfolio Works folder, creative briefs, final process books, packaged project links, and illustration final-file folders. The initial audit indexed 3,153 design files and extracted text from 203 PDFs; a follow-up search also reviewed the ENGR 300 Darksaber proposal, source models, and reference-measurement images. “Missing” means not found or not established in the reviewed material; it does not mean the material cannot exist elsewhere.

Project descriptions summarize the project's own authored brief, defense, or final process deck. They make no claims of measured outcomes, commercial deployment, or client approval without evidence. Student redesigns and fan-art concepts are labeled accordingly. Raw decks, billing pages, and unrelated coursework are not included in the website assets. Selected PDF artwork/process regions were exported and organized under `public/assets/projects/<slug>/`. Originals were read only.
